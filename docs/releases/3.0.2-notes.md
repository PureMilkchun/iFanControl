## 更新内容

1. **睡眠前交还风扇控制权，唤醒后恢复。** 修复部分 Mac 在整机睡眠后风扇仍保持高转速的问题。进入睡眠前，iFanControl 会将风扇交还给 macOS。唤醒后读取最新温度和转速，再恢复原来的风扇曲线或手动转速。
2. **优化菜单栏反馈框。**「告诉开发者」下方的反馈框在浅色模式下使用更柔和的底色和细边框，暗色模式下提示文字更清楚。点击整块即可打开反馈窗口。

**显示器息屏不代表整机进入睡眠。** 如果 Mac 仅关闭显示器，iFanControl 会继续按当前设置控制风扇。风扇交还由整机睡眠触发。

支持 Apple Silicon，要求 macOS 15 或更新版本。若遇兼容性问题，可[下载 2.9.8](https://github.com/PureMilkchun/iFanControl/releases/tag/v2.9.8)。

## What's new

1. **Fan control hands back to macOS before sleep and resumes after wake.** This addresses fans continuing to run at high speed while some Macs are asleep. After wake, iFanControl reads fresh temperature and fan speed data, then restores your fan curve or manual speed.
2. **Refined the menu bar feedback field.** The field below Talk to Dev has a softer fill and thin border in Light Mode and clearer placeholder text in Dark Mode. Click anywhere in the field to open the feedback window.

**The display can turn off while your Mac stays awake.** iFanControl keeps using your current settings while the Mac is awake and hands control back to macOS when the Mac itself goes to sleep.

Apple Silicon with macOS 15 or later. If you encounter compatibility issues, you can [download 2.9.8](https://github.com/PureMilkchun/iFanControl/releases/tag/v2.9.8).
